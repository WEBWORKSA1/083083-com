The 083 prefix, explained

Three countries, three meanings. Here’s exactly what an 083 number tells you — and what it doesn’t.

Key point: a prefix tells you who the range was allocated to, not who carries the number today. Ireland and South Africa both allow mobile number portability.

083 in Ireland (+353)

Irish mobile numbers start with 08 followed by a network-range digit. The 083 range was allocated to Three Ireland; the same prefix was also used by a now-defunct operator that ran on Three’s network. Since numbers can be moved between networks, an 083 caller today might be on any Irish operator.

Format: 083 123 4567 nationally, +353 83 123 4567 internationally.

083 in South Africa (+27)

South African cellphone numbers are ten digits starting with 0. The 083 range is one of the country’s best-known cell prefixes and is listed for MTN. Mobile number portability arrived in November 2006, so the current network may differ from the original allocation.

Format: 083 123 4567 → +27 83 123 4567.

083x in India (+91)

In India, 083 is the start of several long-distance (STD) landline codes covering north Karnataka and Goa — for example Hubballi-Dharwad uses 0836, with smaller towns on five-digit codes such as 08334 and 08335. From abroad you drop the 0: +91 836 ….

How to dial an 083 number from abroad

  1. Dial your country’s exit code (011 from the US/Canada, 00 from most of Europe, Africa and Asia) — or just press + on a mobile.
  2. Add the country code: 353 (Ireland), 27 (South Africa) or 91 (India).
  3. Drop the leading 0 of the number and dial the rest.

Is an 083 call safe?

The prefix itself says nothing about intent — millions of ordinary people use 083 numbers. What matters is behaviour: one-ring missed calls from abroad, pressure to pay now, requests for one-time codes, or a caller ID that looks like your bank. Caller ID can be spoofed, so treat any number — 083 or otherwise — as unverified.

FAQ

Can I find out who owns an 083 number?

Not from us — we don’t publish owner data. Your network or the police can act on harassment complaints.

Does 083 cost more to call?

Calling an 083 mobile costs the normal mobile rate for your plan; calling it internationally costs your international rate. 083 is not a premium-rate prefix in these countries.

Can I get an 083 number for my business?

In Ireland or South Africa you’d get one from an operator that holds 083 stock or by porting one in. Ask our matching service.
