Questions people ask

Is an 083 number a mobile?

In Ireland and South Africa, yes — 083 is a mobile prefix. In India, 083x is a landline STD code. Add the country code to be sure: +353 (Ireland), +27 (South Africa), +91 (India).

Can you tell me who owns a number?

No. We decode what the numbering plan itself reveals — country, range type, pattern. We don’t publish names or personal data about anyone.

Why did I get a missed call from abroad?

It may be a “Wangiri” one-ring scam that hopes you’ll call back a premium-rate line. See the scam-call guide.

How do I get a memorable business number?

Most cloud phone providers sell “golden” or vanity numbers. Use our free matching form and mention the pattern you want.
