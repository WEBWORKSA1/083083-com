Privacy policy

Last updated October 2026

The short version

Our number tools run entirely in your browser — what you type into them is not sent to us. We only receive what you choose to submit in a form. We use Google AdSense to fund the site, which uses cookies.

What we collect

  • Form submissions (contact, quotes, reports, pledges, contest entries, applications): the fields you fill in, the page URL and time. Submissions are relayed by a form-processing service (FormSubmit) to our private inbox.
  • Quote requests: with your consent, shared with up to three matched providers so they can quote you.
  • Preferences: your light/dark theme choice, stored only in your browser.

Advertising & cookies

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland a consent message is shown before personalised ads load.

YouTube

Videos use YouTube’s privacy-enhanced mode and load only when you press play.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete data you submitted via the contact form (topic: Privacy request). We retain form data only as long as needed to answer you or run the service you asked for.

Children

The site is not directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their data.
