The three rules of international dialing

  1. Exit — dial your exit code (011 in North America, 00 almost everywhere else, 0011 in Australia, 010 in Japan) or press + on a mobile.
  2. Enter — dial the destination country code (e.g. 353 Ireland, 27 South Africa, 91 India).
  3. Trim — drop the destination’s trunk prefix (usually 0) and dial the rest.

Saving money on international calls

For frequent international calling, app-based calling over Wi-Fi or a business VoIP plan with international minutes usually beats per-minute carrier rates. Companies with staff or customers abroad can also rent local virtual numbers so callers pay local rates.
